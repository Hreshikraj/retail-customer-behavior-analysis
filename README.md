# Retail Customer Behavior & Shopping Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores retail customer shopping data to understand purchasing patterns, product performance, customer demographics, and recorded purchase revenue.

I used **MySQL** for data validation and analysis and **Tableau Public** to build an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL** – Data validation, cleaning checks, and SQL analysis
- **Tableau Public** – Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Dataset handling and data import

## Dataset Overview

- **Records analysed:** 3,863
- **Columns:** 18
- **SQL table:** `customer_shopping_clean`

The dataset includes customer demographics, purchased items, product categories, purchase amounts, locations, seasons, ratings, subscription status, shipping types, discounts, payment methods, and purchase frequency.

## Analysis Performed

The analysis explored:

1. Customer demographics by gender and age group
2. Recorded purchase revenue by product category
3. Purchase patterns across seasons
4. Top-performing products by recorded purchase amount
5. Revenue distribution across locations
6. Payment method analysis
7. Subscription and discount comparisons
8. Shipping type and purchase frequency analysis
9. Purchase amount segmentation into Low, Medium, and High groups

## Key Findings

- **Total recorded purchase amount:** $230,629
- **Total records:** 3,863
- **Gender distribution:** Male records represented approximately 67.7% of the dataset.
- **Leading category:** Clothing recorded the highest purchase amount at $102,968.
- **Seasonal comparison:** Fall had the highest recorded purchase amount
-  Seasonal comparison: Fall had the highest recorded purchase amount at $59,620.
